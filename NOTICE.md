# Notice

## Icon

The icon in `custom_components/autoshutterscontrol/brand/` (`icon.png`, `icon@2x.png`, `dark_icon.png`,
`dark_icon@2x.png`) is made of two parts from [FHEM](https://fhem.de/), to show where AutoShuttersControl
comes from:

- The shutter is the icon `fts_shutter_70` from the OpenAutomation icon set as shipped with FHEM
  (`www/images/openautomation/fts_shutter_70.svg`).
  - License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Germany (CC BY-SA 3.0 DE)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/de/)
  - Changes: placed on a square canvas, white instead of black in the dark variant, rendered as PNG
    (256 × 256 and 512 × 512).
- The badge shows the FHEM logo (`www/images/default/fhemicon.png` in the FHEM repository) on a white circle
  with a green ring. The logo belongs to the FHEM project and is used to name it as the origin of
  AutoShuttersControl.

The icon is under CC BY-SA 3.0 DE as far as it contains the shutter icon. It is not covered by the
GPL-2.0-or-later license of the code.
